Featured Memory

Draco Glide - Wild Kratts

Read more

One of my favorite games from the Wild Kratts franchise, Draco Glide was simple enough to understand while still becoming challenging in its later levels. It struck a nice balance where I could still complete it as a kid without it feeling too easy.

I especially liked how smooth and responsive the controls felt, which made gliding around as the dragon really satisfying. The game also had a good variety of levels and challenges that kept me engaged for a long time, including flying through smaller hoops and avoiding predators while airborne.
