Why I Made This

PBS Kids was a large part of my childhood, especially its website and the games that came with many of the shows I watched.

Years later, I wanted to revisit some of those games and see what I actually remembered about them. Some are still playable today, while others seem to have disappeared.

This site is my personal collection of those memories, along with thoughts on the games, shows, artwork, sounds, and website designs that stuck with me.

About the Project

Not a Review Site

I'm not trying to score or professionally review these games. I'm more interested in what it feels like to return to something I played as a kid.

Built From Memory

Some things on this site can still be played or found online. Others are based mostly on what I remember from years ago.

A G-Rated Experiment

This site was also made as a design challenge: creating something completely all-ages while still giving it a strong personality and visual identity.
